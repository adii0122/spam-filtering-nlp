End-to-End NLP Pipeline

How text transforms from raw characters into numerical vectors and machine learning predictions.

Step 01
Text Cleaning
Strips punctuation, handles encoding, and casts to lowercase.
Step 02
Tokenization
Segments character strings into individual semantic tokens.
Step 03
Stopwords Filter
Removes non-informative English functional words (NLTK corpus).
Step 04
Snowball Stemmer
Reduces inflectional variants to base root words.
Step 05
TF-IDF & Model
Computes term frequency-inverse document frequency matrices.
